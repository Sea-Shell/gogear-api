# Requirements change log

## 2026-11-22

- **Creation (backup before schema migrations):** From the owner's decision RD3 ([release and deployment anchor](/docs/anchors/release-and-deployment.md)) and the advisory of the milestone A review (Loom QA `bd9e4664`: the 000011 release cannot be undone by redeploying), new [BR-038](/docs/requirements/requirements/br-038-backup-before-schema-migration.md) is added. Before applying pending migrations, the application makes and checks a consistent backup by itself. It covers storage location, retention of three backups, no backup when nothing is pending or on a first start, refusing to migrate when the backup fails, restore without copying files by hand, and SQLite-only scope. Provisional defaults P-37 to P-40; open owner questions Q-8 (backup failure) and Q-9 (automatic restore after a failed migration). It is a precondition for merging milestone A. [BR-022](/docs/requirements/requirements/br-022-merge-to-main-releases-and-deploys.md) gets a related link, and the [index](/docs/requirements/index.md) a summary, a row, the defaults and the questions. Not implemented.
